# FTAPI Enterprise Platform Validation

The **FTAPI Enterprise Platform** served as the primary real-world testbed for the [Automating DDD research](ddd-prompting-framework.md).

## Validation Scope
The researchers applied the [Prompting Framework](ddd-prompting-framework.md) to the requirements of FTAPI, a major secure data communication platform.

## Key Outcomes from Validation
- **Discovery Success:** The model-generated Ubiquitous Language and Event Storming artifacts were rated as "highly valuable" by FTAPI's architects.
- **Mapping Failure:** The low-level technical mappings (API and DB designs) for the FTAPI platform were deemed "impractical for production" due to accumulated errors.
- **Conclusion:** The validation proved that AI excels at **Strategic Architectural Discovery** but currently falls short at **Tactical Implementation Design**.
