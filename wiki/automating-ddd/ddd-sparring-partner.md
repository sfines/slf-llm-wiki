# Sparring Partner Paradigm

The **Sparring Partner Paradigm** is a philosophical approach to using AI in architectural design, as proposed in the [Automating DDD research](../../raw/automating-ddd-paper.md).

## Core Concept
Instead of trying to replace the architect with a fully autonomous system, the AI is used as a **"Sparring Partner"** to:
- **Challenge Assumptions:** Suggesting alternative bounded contexts or event sequences.
- **Reduce Overhead:** Automating the tedious documentation of Ubiquitous Language and glossary terms.
- **Identify Gaps:** Pointing out missing events or inconsistent naming in the domain model.

## Human-Centricity
The paradigm emphasizes that **Human Architectural Expertise** remains critical for making the final trade-offs and correcting the [Error Propagation](ddd-error-propagation.md) inherent in LLM-driven design.
