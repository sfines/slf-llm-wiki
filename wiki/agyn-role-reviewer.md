# Agent Role: Reviewer

The **Reviewer** is a specialized role within the [Agyn Framework](agyn-framework.md) responsible for quality assurance and correctness validation.

## Key Responsibilities
- **Logic Validation:** Ensuring the [Implementer's](agyn-role-implementer.md) changes actually solve the reported issue.
- **Regression Check:** Verifying that no new bugs or performance issues were introduced.
- **Coding Standards Enforcement:** Checking for adherence to established project conventions.
- **Feedback Generation:** Providing specific, actionable critiques back to the Implementer.

## Role as a "Human-Mimic"
The Reviewer acts as the "checks and balances" mechanism of the [Team-Based SE](team-based-software-engineering.md) paradigm, mimicking the role of a senior human developer in a human engineering team.
