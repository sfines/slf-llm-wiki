# Domain-Driven Design (DDD)

Domain-Driven Design is a software development approach that focuses on modeling software to match a complex domain according to input from that domain's experts.

## Core Concepts
- **[Bounded Contexts](bounded-contexts-in-mas.md):** A central pattern in DDD; it is the focus of Strategic Design.
- **[Ubiquitous Language](ubiquitous-language-for-ai.md):** A language structured around the domain model and used by all team members to connect all the activities of the team with the software.
- **Entities and Aggregates:** Structural patterns for managing state and consistency.

## Relevance to Agents
[Agentic DDD](agentic-ddd-overview.md) adapts these principles to the design of autonomous AI systems. Furthermore, research into **[Automating Domain-Driven Design](ddd-prompting-framework.md)** explores how LLMs can be used as a **[Sparring Partner](ddd-sparring-partner.md)** to generate artifacts like **[Automated Event Storming](ddd-event-storming-simulation.md)** and **[Bounded Context Identification](ddd-bounded-context-identification.md)**.

## Challenges in Automation
A critical finding in DDD automation is **[Error Propagation](ddd-error-propagation.md)**, where minor inaccuracies in the early stages of a design chain lead to impractical technical outputs.
