# APWA Architecture

The **APWA Architecture** is designed for high-throughput, distributed reasoning in [Multi-Agent Systems](multi-agent-systems.md). It is structured to avoid the coordination bottlenecks typical of tightly coupled agent chains.

## Layered Design
1.  **Orchestration Layer:** Receives the global query and initiates the [Decomposition process](apwa-dynamic-decomposition.md).
2.  **Decomposition Layer (The Decomposer):** Analyzes the task for "parallelism potential" and generates independent [Subproblem Specifications](apwa-non-interfering-subproblems.md).
3.  **Worker Layer (Parallel Workers):** A cluster of independent reasoning agents that execute the subproblems. These workers are isolated and do not communicate with each other during the execution phase.
4.  **Aggregation Layer:** Collects the results from all workers and synthesizes the final response to the user.

## Communication Model: Zero-Communication Execution
A critical feature of the APWA architecture is the **Zero-Communication execution phase**. By ensuring that subproblems are truly independent, APWA eliminates the need for expensive synchronization or "reasoning loops" between workers. This is the primary driver of its [85% efficiency gain](apwa-evaluation-results.md).

## Resource Management
APWA utilizes a **Dynamic Worker Pool**. Reasoning resources (LLM instances) can be scaled up or down based on the number of subproblems generated by the Decomposer, allowing for [Linear Scalability](apwa-scaling-performance.md).
