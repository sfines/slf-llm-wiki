# Supply Chain Agent Authorization (Use Case)

In high-assurance industries like aerospace and defense, components move across vast networks of tiered suppliers. Autonomous agents follow these components, collecting and presenting attestations without exposing proprietary data.

## Evidence Integrity and Bounded Presentation

This use case focuses on cryptographic provenance, evidence integrity, and scoped attestation flows. Agents must carry authorization limiting which compliance evidence they can present, to which auditor roles, and for which specific namespaces.

## Long-Horizon Auditability

Unlike fast-paced claims, supply chain interactions have a slower tempo but demand long-horizon auditability. An agent's authority and evidentiary chain must withstand regulatory scrutiny months or years after the action occurred.
