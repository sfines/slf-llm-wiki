# Organizational Process Modeling for Agents

**Organizational Process Modeling** is the practice of designing [Multi-Agent Systems](multi-agent-systems.md) to function as a digital replica of a human organization.

## The Agyn Thesis
The [Agyn research](agyn-framework.md) argues that for complex software engineering, the **organizational structure** is as important as the model's reasoning capability.

## Key Modeling Elements
- **Roles:** Defining specialized functions ([Coordinator](agyn-role-coordinator.md), [Researcher](agyn-role-researcher.md), etc.).
- **Process:** Defining the steps and methodology the roles must follow.
- **Environment:** Providing the necessary tools and [Sandboxes](agyn-sandbox-environments.md) for the roles to function.
- **Communication:** Establishing the protocols for interaction.

## Shift in Paradigm
This approach represents a shift from "Prompt Engineering" (optimizing the model's input) to "Organizational Engineering" (optimizing the system's structure).
