# DataAlchemy Framework

DataAlchemy is an isolated and controlled experimental environment designed to train Large Language Models (LLMs) from scratch and systematically probe their Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning capabilities.

By utilizing customizable basic atoms and specific transformation rules (like ROT transformations or Cyclic Position Shifts), DataAlchemy creates synthetic datasets that allow researchers to tightly control distribution conditions. This removes the interference of complex patterns learned during massive real-world pre-training, enabling rigorous and precise investigation of how distribution shifts across tasks, sequence lengths, and prompt formats impact CoT reasoning.
