# CoT Format Generalization

CoT Format Generalization explores a model's sensitivity to surface-level variations in prompt formulation and query structure.

If CoT were a robust, intrinsic reasoning engine, minor syntactic changes to how a question is asked should not derail the logic. However, studies show that CoT is highly susceptible to format distributional shifts. Altering the prompt template, inserting irrelevant distractor clauses, or changing symbolic representations causes sharp performance drops. This highlights that CoT relies heavily on familiar templates and surface-level semantic clues rather than sound logical procedures.
