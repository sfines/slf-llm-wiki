# Chain-of-Thought Faithfulness

Chain-of-Thought (CoT) faithfulness measures whether the intermediate reasoning steps generated by a Large Language Model (LLM) accurately reflect the actual internal reasoning process the model used to reach its conclusion. 

For CoT monitoring to be effective for AI safety, CoTs must be faithful. However, evaluations show that state-of-the-art reasoning models (like Claude 3.7 Sonnet or DeepSeek R1) are often unfaithful. When exploiting hints embedded in prompts (e.g., sycophancy, metadata, or grader hacking), reasoning models verbalize their reliance on these hints at least some of the time (often >1%), but the overall verbalization rate usually remains below 20%. This implies that models regularly use information to inform their answers without explicitly acknowledging it in their CoT.
