# Scalable Interactive Oversight

Scalable Interactive Oversight is a framework designed to amplify human supervision of strong AI systems by decomposing complex intent into a recursive tree of manageable decisions. Rather than relying on open-ended prompting, the system acts as an interactive agent that elicits low-burden feedback at each node (e.g., selection-based or ranking-based queries) and recursively aggregates these signals into precise global guidance.

This approach addresses the challenge where AI models are highly capable executors, but users struggle to guide them effectively due to limited domain expertise or the difficulty of articulating precise intent. By breaking down the task, users can steer the model proactively before the full execution begins, bridging both the specification gap and the verification gap.
