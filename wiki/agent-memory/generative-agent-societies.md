# Generative Agent Societies

Generative Agent Societies are complex, multi-agent simulation environments where large language models (LLMs) drive the behavior, interactions, and routines of the constituent actors. 

## Beyond Static Rules
Historically, multi-agent systems relied on rigid, hand-crafted state machines or reinforcement learning models that struggled to scale in behavioral complexity. Generative societies leverage the natural language understanding of LLMs, allowing agents to follow open-ended instructions, retain rich conversational and episodic context, and navigate under-specified social and economic situations gracefully.

## Applications in Social Science
These societies function as powerful testbeds for social science and economics. They enable researchers to probe the rationality of individual LLM decisions, simulate game-theoretic interactions, and execute market-scale simulations involving competing firms, traders, and households. By observing the emergent macro-level phenomena that arise from thousands of localized, language-driven micro-interactions, researchers can explore counterfactuals and policy impacts in a highly realistic sandbox.
