# Household vs Firm Agent Roles

In macroeconomic simulations like EconAI, agent populations are typically bifurcated into distinct roles—principally households and firms—to accurately model the duality of the economy.

## Household Agents
Households represent the microeconomic foundation of the simulation. Each household agent is assigned a persona with specific professions, skills, and experiences. Their primary decision loop revolves around managing their personal wealth through two key choices: supplying labor (willingness to work) and generating demand (propensity to consume).

## Firm Agents
Firms operate at the macroeconomic layer, driving production and market strategy. They utilize the labor supplied by households (often modeled via functions like Cobb-Douglas) to produce goods. Firms must navigate market phenomena such as competition, cooperation, price adjustments, and wage setting based on the aggregate demand generated by the households.

Together, the push-and-pull between household labor/consumption and firm production/pricing creates the foundational closed-loop system necessary for emergent macroeconomic behaviors.
