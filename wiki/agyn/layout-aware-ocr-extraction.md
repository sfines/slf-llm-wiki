# Layout-Aware OCR Extraction

Layout-Aware OCR Extraction is crucial for parsing unstructured or legacy financial documents, such as scanned balance sheets and income statements, without losing their inherent tabular structure. This technology bridges the gap between raw document retrieval and structured downstream analysis.

## Structural Preservation

- Unlike legacy OCR engines that flatten text into a continuous stream, layout-aware parsers maintain the spatial relationships of rows and columns.
- Enables downstream AI agents to reliably map extracted text into standardized financial metrics (e.g., Assets, Liabilities, Revenue, EBIT).

## Auditable Citations

- Extracts not just the raw data but also the exact page numbers and document coordinates, preserving source provenance for human verification.
