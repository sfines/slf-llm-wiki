# Agent Role: Coordinator

The **Coordinator** is a specialized role within the [Agyn Framework](agyn-framework.md) responsible for managing the overall organizational process.

## Key Responsibilities
- **Workflow Management:** Directing the sequence of actions across the agent team (Analysis -> Spec -> Impl -> Review).
- **Task Assignment:** Breaking down high-level issues into specific sub-tasks for [Researchers](agyn-role-researcher.md) and [Implementers](agyn-role-implementer.md).
- **Conflict Resolution:** Mediating between agents when reasoning paths diverge.
- **Goal Tracking:** Ensuring the team remains focused on the primary objective and doesn't get lost in minor details.

## Role in Team Dynamics
The Coordinator acts as the "manager" of the [Autonomous Team](team-based-software-engineering.md), ensuring that the [Shared Methodology](agyn-framework.md#core-philosophy) is followed by all members.
