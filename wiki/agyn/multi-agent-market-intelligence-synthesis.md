# Multi-Agent Market Intelligence Synthesis

Multi-Agent Market Intelligence Synthesis occurs at the convergence point of a multi-agent workflow. It is the crucial stage where diverse intelligence streams—such as financial data, competitor analysis, and news—are aggregated into coherent, strategic evaluations.

## Aggregation Layers

- **Research Note**: Consolidates sector, competition, news, signals, and financial data to surface recent strategic developments and potential blind spots.
- **Executive Summary**: Synthesizes the note to generate attractiveness scores (e.g., market timing, product differentiation) and formulates actionable 30-to-180-day recommendations for both the fund and the portfolio company.

## Contextual Alignment

- Dynamically tailors the generated output for different stakeholders.
- Capable of producing internal memos for venture capital analysts alongside external-facing overviews suitable for startup founders.
