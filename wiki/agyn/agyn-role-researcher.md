# Agent Role: Researcher

The **Researcher** is a specialized role within the [Agyn Framework](agyn-framework.md) focused on codebase analysis and problem discovery.

## Key Responsibilities
- **Codebase Exploration:** Navigating large repositories to understand the architecture and identify relevant files.
- **Issue Analysis:** Breaking down bug reports or feature requests to understand the root cause.
- **Technical Specification:** Documenting the required changes and potential side effects for the [Implementer](agyn-role-implementer.md).
- **Knowledge Retrieval:** Using tools like [RAG](../advanced-retrieval/rag.md) or [Pseudo-Knowledge Graphs](../advanced-retrieval/pseudo-knowledge-graph.md) to gather external documentation.

## Importance in Agyn
By isolating research into a dedicated role, Agyn prevents the [Implementer](agyn-role-implementer.md) from being overwhelmed with "discovery" work, leading to more precise and less error-prone code changes.
