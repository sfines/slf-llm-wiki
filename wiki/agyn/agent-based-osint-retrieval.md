# Agent-Based OSINT Retrieval

Agent-Based OSINT (Open-Source Intelligence) Retrieval deploys specialized LLM agents to scan the web for real-time market signals. By retrieving live data, this strategy overcomes the static knowledge limitations and training data cut-offs of pre-trained language models.

## Intelligence Streams

- **Source Mapping**: Dynamically identifying the most reliable sector-specific portals, databases, and news aggregators for a given target company.
- **Strategic Scanning**: Extracting actionable investment signals, such as recent funding rounds, corporate partnerships, and product launches.
- **Competitive Mapping**: Classifying identified competitors into tiers (e.g., direct, adjacent, niche innovators) based on real-time market data.
