# Multi-Agent Due Diligence Framework

A Multi-Agent Due Diligence Framework utilizes autonomous AI agents to automate corporate research and market analysis, significantly accelerating the venture capital evaluation process. By distributing specialized tasks among different agents, it overcomes the limitations of single-prompt interactions and enables robust decision-making.

## Core Components

- **Data Intake**: Initializing context through a single trigger mechanism that maps target company attributes.
- **Market Intelligence**: Querying real-time sources to extract market sizing, trends, and competitive landscapes.
- **Financial Retrieval**: Programmatically extracting official filings and applying layout-aware OCR for structured financial metrics.
- **Synthesis**: Aggregating insights into a comprehensive strategic research note and executive summary.
