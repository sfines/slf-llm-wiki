# Guided Reflection in LLMs

Guided reflection enhances standard LLM self-reflection by avoiding context-length limitations and providing a structured diagnosis of errors. 

## Mechanism
Rather than forcing an LLM to reflect blindly on a massive trajectory, guided reflection first summarizes the trajectory into three components: 
1. Key reasoning steps.
2. The final answer.
3. Key areas for improvement.

The Critic module then performs its reflection based on these targeted dimensions. This structured approach allows the reflection agent to transcend superficial error correction and target precise logical, strategic, and knowledge gaps generated by the Solver phase.
