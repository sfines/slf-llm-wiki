# Autonomous Agents

Autonomous Agents are entities that perceive their environment, reason about how to achieve goals, and take actions using tools or APIs to change their environment.

## Characteristics
- **Autonomy:** Operates without constant human intervention.
- **Reactivity:** Responds to changes in the environment or new events.
- **Proactivity:** Takes initiative to achieve goals.
- **Social Ability:** Interacts with other agents or humans.
