# Organizational Design for Agents

**Organizational Design for Agents** is the study and practice of designing the structures, roles, and processes that enable multiple [Autonomous Agents](autonomous-agents.md) to work together effectively.

## Shift from Model to Process
Traditional AI research focuses on the **Model** (increasing parameters, better reasoning). Organizational design focuses on the **Process**—how those models are organized into a system. As suggested by the [Agyn Paper](../raw/agyn-paper.md), future progress in autonomous engineering may depend as much on organizational design as on model improvements.

## Design Dimensions
- **Topology:** The communication structure of the team (Centralized vs. Decentralized vs. Hybrid).
- **Incentives:** How agent "success" is defined and rewarded within the system.
- **Workflow:** The sequence of actions and hand-offs required to complete a task.
- **Governance:** The rules and constraints that prevent agents from deviating from the shared goal.

## Key Principles
- **[Role Separation](team-based-software-engineering.md):** Avoiding the "Generalist" trap by creating highly specialized units.
- **Observability:** Ensuring the system can be monitored and audited by humans (and other agents).
- **Adaptability:** Allowing the organization to evolve its structure in response to performance feedback (see [Agentic Harness Engineering](agentic-harness-engineering.md)).
