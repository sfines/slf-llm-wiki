# ACE Process: Curation

**Curation** is the final stage of the [Agentic Context Engineering (ACE)](agentic-context-engineering.md) modular process.

## Role in Context Evolution
Curation is the process of selecting and organizing the most effective strategies and evidence into the final "evolved playbook" or system prompt.

## Selection Criteria
- **Empirical Success:** Prioritizing strategies that have been verified through [Reflection](ace-process-reflection.md).
- **Redundancy Removal:** Consolidating overlapping instructions to maintain efficiency.
- **Hierarchical Organization:** Arranging information so that it is most accessible to the model during execution.

## Maintaining Long-Context Utility
ACE curation is designed to scale with long-context models, ensuring that the "evolved context" remains organized and high-signal even as it grows in size.
