# Agent Capability Saturation

Agent capability saturation describes the phenomenon where multi-agent coordination yields diminishing or even negative returns once the baseline intelligence of a single-agent system exceeds a certain threshold.

## The Saturation Threshold

Research indicates that when a single-agent system approaches or exceeds roughly 45% accuracy on a task, the cost of coordinating multiple agents begins to outweigh the benefits. 

While multi-agent systems can boost performance for weaker foundation models by combining diverse perspectives, frontier models with strong context windows, advanced tool use, and self-reflection capabilities often perform better alone. In these high-capability scenarios, the communication overhead and information fragmentation inherent in multi-agent collaboration become a net cost, degrading overall system performance.
