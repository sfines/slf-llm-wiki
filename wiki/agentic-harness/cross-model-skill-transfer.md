# Cross-Model Skill Transfer

Cross-Model Skill Transfer assesses the generalizability of synthesized inference-time skills by evaluating whether a skill generated by one source model can successfully improve the performance of a distinct target evaluator model.

## Evaluating Skill Generalizability

Because inference-time skills are human-readable prompt interventions rather than model-specific weight updates, they have the potential to be portable. Research demonstrates that high-quality skills can often transfer positively to other models, although the degree of success depends heavily on the capabilities of both the source and target agents.

## Source vs. Evaluator Dynamics

Transferability is not strictly correlated with the raw power of the source model. Specific models might generate highly optimal skills for certain domains (like coding or tool use) that transfer well to significantly different model architectures, highlighting the modularity of declarative agent knowledge.
